What this is

A Year 12 IB CAS group project at Dhirubhai Ambani International School, Mumbai, academic year 2026–27. It has two connected parts: technology-literacy sessions through September and October, and an AI-enabled, social-impact hackathon that closes the project in late October.

Learn

The education programme

Technology-literacy induction sessions. Offline sessions for NGO and community groups cover topics like digital financial literacy and cybersecurity, starting from the basics. Online preparation sessions get hackathon participants ready with AI literacy, vibe coding and pitching.

  • Starts September 2026
  • 6–8 induction sessions
  • Offline and online

The hackathon

The final event. Teams work on a social problem linked to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, build a working prototype with any tools they like, AI included, and pitch it to judges.

  • 24–25 October 2026 Dates tentative
  • Online (planned) on Discord
  • Teams of up to 4
