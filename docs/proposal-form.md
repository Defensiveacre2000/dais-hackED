#  Timeline and Hackathon Format:

-   **Project structure:** A technology-literacy programme culminating
    in an AI-enabled, social-impact hackathon.

-   **Literacy programme:**

    -   **Start:** Sessions are expected to begin in September 2026.

    -   **Working scope:** Approximately 6-8 sessions were discussed;
        the exact number and final topic list remain to be confirmed.

> Offline sessions are proposed for approved NGO/community groups, with
> online preparation sessions for hackathon participants.

-   **Tentative hackathon dates:** 24-25 October 2026.

-   **Format:** An online event is strongly preferred, likely using
    Discord + Hackerank; a 24-hour duration remains under consideration.

# Participant Structure

-   **Teams:** Maximum 4 participants per team.

-   **Divisions:** Junior (Grade 10 and below; exact lower boundary to
    be confirmed) and Senior (Grade 11 and above; possible college
    inclusion to be confirmed).

# Hackathon Flow and Deliverables

-   **Opening:** Guest speaker, challenge/theme briefing, and Q&A.

-   **Build period:** AI use is permitted. Teams develop a
    technology-based solution to a social problem or SDG, with organizer
    support through Discord.

**Submission:** MVP + pitch/deck + explanation of implementation.

**Judging:** Overall solution quality and feasibility, implementation
thinking, prototype, and presentation. The exact rubric and judges
remain to be finalized.

**Challenge guidance:** Younger participants will receive more
structured problem statements; older participants will receive a broader
social-impact/SDG theme.

# CAS Stages

### **Investigation**

-   **Identify the Need, Initial Exploration**: The group reviewed the
    previous competitive-programming model in light of widespread AI
    use, likely participant interest, and the organizing team\'s current
    experience.

-   The discussion identified a need for accessible technology literacy
    and opportunities to apply AI and software skills to social
    challenges and SDGs. This led to a two-part project: literacy
    sessions followed by a final hackathon.

### **Preparation**

-   **Developing a Plan**: Define a limited workshop programme covering
    the agreed core areas: AI literacy, basic coding/vibe coding,
    digital financial literacy and cybersecurity, and pitching and
    solution-development skills. Adapt the content to the audience\'s
    prior technical knowledge.

-   For the hackathon, finalize the age divisions, social-impact problem
    statements, rules, judging rubric, judges, registration process,
    Discord structure, exact schedule, and participant information
    packet. Obtain school/CAS and NGO/community approval before offline
    sessions.

### **Action**

-   **Implementation**: Run offline literacy sessions for approved
    NGO/community audiences and online preparation sessions for
    hackathon participants, beginning in September. Start with basic
    concepts where needed and adapt the material to the audience.

-   **Hackathon**: Conduct the final AI-enabled, social-impact event
    online, tentatively on 24-25 October 2026. Open with a guest speaker
    and theme briefing, support teams on Discord during the build
    period, collect MVPs and pitches, and complete judging.
