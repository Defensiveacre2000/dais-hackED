The hackathon

A 24-hour online hackathon where teams build technology for a real social problem, run by Year 12 students at Dhirubhai Ambani International School, Mumbai as their IB CAS project.

The challenge

The UN Sustainable Development Goals you'll work on are revealed on the day of the hackathon. That's the challenge: pick up the problem, build a solution in 24 hours, and pitch it.

What you submit

A working prototype (MVP), a short pitch deck, and an explanation of how your solution would be put into practice.

Who can enter

  • Junior Grades 6 to 10
  • Senior Grade 11, 12 and college students

Teams

Up to 4 people per team. Solo entries are welcome.

AI and vibe coding

AI tools and vibe coding are allowed, with no restrictions.

Where it happens

The whole event is online, so you can take part from anywhere. Registration details will be posted on this page.

6 induction sessions

Before the hackathon, the team runs technology-literacy sessions from September 2026: online sessions that prepare participants, and offline sessions with NGO and community groups.
